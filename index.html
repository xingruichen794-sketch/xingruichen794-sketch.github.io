Xingrui Chen

I am a master's student in Mechanical Engineering at Duke University, concentrating in robotics. My work focuses on robotic manipulation and mechanical design.

Before Duke, I received my B.Eng. in Mechanical Engineering from Hefei University of Technology, where I led the Formula Student aerodynamics and bodywork team and designed an RV reducer for a humanoid waist joint. I develop and evaluate robotic systems through simulation and hardware experiments, connecting perception and planning with physical execution.
